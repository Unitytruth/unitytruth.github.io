Zero Tolerance of Anger

A practical character-development module focused on recognising anger early, choosing responsible responses, protecting relationships and developing habits of calm, self-control and constructive communication.

LESSON 01

Understanding Anger

Recognise anger as an emotional response, identify common triggers and understand the difference between feeling anger and acting harmfully.

LESSON 02

Recognising the Warning Signs

Learn to notice physical, emotional and behavioural signals early so that a pause can happen before an impulsive response.

LESSON 03

The Pause Before the Response

Practise stopping, breathing, stepping away and choosing a safe response rather than reacting in the heat of the moment.

LESSON 04

Words, Conflict & Relationships

Develop respectful communication, active listening and boundaries while avoiding insults, threats, intimidation and retaliation.

LESSON 05

Forgiveness & Responsibility

Learn to take responsibility for harmful behaviour, make appropriate amends and work towards healthier patterns without excusing abuse.

LESSON 06

A Life of Self-Control

Create a personal anger-management plan built around reflection, healthy routines, communication, support and consistent practice.

🕊️

The Zero-Anger Commitment

Our principle: We do not promote violence, intimidation, threats, humiliation or destructive retaliation.

Our practice: Pause before reacting, speak respectfully, step away when necessary and seek constructive help when conflict becomes difficult.

Safety first: If there is an immediate risk of violence or someone is in danger, move to a safe place and contact appropriate emergency or professional support.

UNITY HIDDEN TRUTH INSTITUTE • FULL COURSE

ANGER: WHY, HOW, WHEN, WHAT & WHERE

A structured course for understanding anger from its causes and triggers to its expression, consequences, locations and contexts — with practical methods for self-control, communication, reconciliation and safe intervention.

🧭

The Five Core Questions

  • WHY? Why does anger arise? Explore needs, expectations, perceived injustice, frustration, fear, hurt, stress and learned responses.
  • HOW? How does anger develop and show itself? Examine thoughts, body signals, words, behaviour, escalation and coping patterns.
  • WHEN? When does anger become stronger? Identify triggers, timing, accumulated stress, repeated conflict and moments requiring a deliberate pause.
  • WHAT? What is anger, what is it not, and what can it produce? Distinguish emotion from harmful action and examine consequences.
  • WHERE? Where does anger appear? Explore home, workplace, school, relationships, online spaces, public places and internal/private experiences.

Why Do We Become Angry?

Causes and contributing factors: unmet expectations, perceived disrespect, injustice, disappointment, fear, hurt, frustration, exhaustion, stress, jealousy and unresolved conflict.

⚠️

Safety Principle

This course does not encourage suppressing legitimate concerns or tolerating abuse. The objective is responsible management of anger and behaviour. If someone is at immediate risk of violence, the priority is to reach safety and obtain appropriate emergency or professional assistance.

👑

The Kingdom Practice

Know: Learn the attributes of God.

Understand: Discover what each attribute means.

Practise: Apply the attribute in daily situations.

Embody: Develop it as a quality of character.

Express: Let the quality become visible through conduct and service.

“Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord… but he that doeth the will of my Father.” — Matthew 7:21 (KJV)

Metaphysical Kingdom Principle

The Kingdom of God may be explored metaphysically as the inner and lived government of divine truth, love, wisdom, righteousness, peace and compassion. This is presented as an interpretive framework alongside the biblical text, not as a replacement for the wider biblical context.
